Our story

Casa Verde started in 2012 as a family food stand at the farmers’ market, with one grill, three taco recipes, and a hand-painted sign. Twelve years later we’re still doing it the same way: tortillas pressed every morning, salsas made from scratch, and margaritas squeezed to order. Everything on the menu is a family recipe — and the green sauce is still a secret.
