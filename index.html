Good to know

Do I need to buy a domain?

Your site can go live on a free address first. If you want your own domain (like yourbusiness.com), it costs about $10–15 a year from the registrar — you pay that directly, and I set it up for you.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, address, hours, phone, a list of services or your menu, and any photos or logo you have. No photos? I can work with stock images or a clean text layout.

How do I pay?

50% to start, 50% when the site is live. I accept USDC (Solana). Other payment options are available on request — ask when you email.

What if I don’t like the first draft?

Every package includes revisions (see the pricing above). If we can’t get it right after the included revisions, we talk it through before any extra work — no surprise charges.
