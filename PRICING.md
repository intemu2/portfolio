## Tiers (one-time payment, USD)

### Starter — $25 — One-page landing site
Best for: a business with no website at all that needs to be found on Google.
- 1 page, mobile-friendly, fast loading
- Business name, what you offer, hours, phone, address
- Google Maps embed + contact info
- Basic search setup (page title, description, Google Business Profile link)
- 1 round of revisions
- Free hosting setup (GitHub Pages / Netlify free tier)
- Ready in ~3 days

Why $25: the cheapest possible "yes". A small business owner can say yes without thinking. Comparable to the cheapest Fiverr gigs, but sold direct (no 20% platform cut). The point is the first sale, not the margin.

### Refresh — $50 — Fix & improve your current site
Best for: a business whose site looks dated, loads slowly, or breaks on phones.
- Faster loading (image and code cleanup)
- Mobile-friendly layout fixes
- Updated text, hours, and photos
- Basic search improvements
- 1 round of revisions
- Ready in ~5 days

Why $50: the Tumbleweed Café case — a business that already owns a domain but has an empty "Launching Soon" page. A $50 fix is an easy sell because the owner already paid for the domain and wants it used. More work than a landing page, less than a full site.

### Business — $75 — Full multi-page website
Best for: a business that wants a complete online presence.
- Up to 5 pages (home, about, menu/services, gallery, contact)
- Google Maps embed + contact form
- Menu / services / photo gallery
- Basic search setup (page titles, descriptions, structured data)
- 2 rounds of revisions
- Free hosting setup (GitHub Pages / Netlify free tier)
- Ready in ~7 days

Why $75: still far below US market rates (a comparable small-business site from a US freelancer typically starts at $300–500), but 3× the Starter price, so it rewards bigger jobs. Positioned as the "full" option for owners who want everything done at once.
